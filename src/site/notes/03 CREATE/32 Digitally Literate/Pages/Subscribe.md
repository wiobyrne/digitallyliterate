# Subscribe

Digitally Literate is a weekly letter about the forces shaping how we read,
write, learn, and live in digital spaces.

Issues move across artificial intelligence, education, literacy, privacy,
platforms, and the open web. I use the letter to share what I am reading, work through an argument, and connect current events to questions that deserve more time.

It has run since 2015. This is issue 448 and counting.

## Read it by email

[Subscribe on Substack](https://digitallyliterate.substack.com/) to get each issue
in your inbox the week it goes out. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Follow by RSS

Point your reader at
[digitallyliterate.substack.com/feed](https://digitallyliterate.substack.com/feed).
No email address required.

## Read on the web

The [complete archive](/newsletter/) is open without a subscription — every issue
back to 2015. The web version is the fuller one: it carries the links, tags, and
connections to everything else in the garden that the emailed version leaves out.

New here? [Start here](/start-here/).
