# Subscribe

Digitally Literate is a weekly newsletter and connected notebook for anyone trying to make sense of AI and the digital world, and keep some power over it.

Issues move across artificial intelligence, education, literacy, privacy, platforms, and the open web. I use the letter to share what I am reading, work through an argument, and connect current events to questions that deserve more time.

It has run since 2015. 450+ issues and counting.

## Read it by email

[Subscribe on Substack](https://digitallyliterate.substack.com/) to get each issue in your inbox the week it goes out. You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Follow by RSS

Point your reader at [digitallyliterate.net/feed.xml](https://digitallyliterate.net/feed.xml). No email address required.

## Read on the web

The [complete archive](/newsletter/) is open without a subscription — every issue back to 2015. The web version is the fuller one: it carries the links, tags, and connections to everything else in the garden that the emailed version leaves out.
